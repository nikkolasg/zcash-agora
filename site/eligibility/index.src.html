Who can vote

Anyone with a valid passport that has a chip, from any country, of any age. One person, one vote, in every poll on this ledger.

The rule

  • A passport with a chip, not expired on the day you register. Identity cards and residence permits do not work yet.
  • No minimum age and no nationality rule. The poll asks what people think, not which people.
  • The ledger keeps a number unique to your passport, so it cannot register twice. It never receives your name, nationality, birth date or photo.

Why a passport

A count of people needs a way to tell one person from two accounts. Forum handles, e-mails and wallet addresses can be made by the dozen. A passport is issued once per person by a state, carries that state's signature, and a phone can check it. With ZKPassport the check and the proof happen on your phone. What leaves it is a proof that the rule holds and a number derived from the document. Nothing else.

What it costs

  • Some people cannot take part. No chip passport, no NFC phone, or a country that does not publish its signing certificates means no vote. That exclusion is real, and it falls unevenly on the world.
  • Registering takes a face match. The unique number is salted by independent servers, so the state that issued your passport cannot recompute it and learn that you registered. ZKPassport asks for a selfie in that mode, checked on your phone against the passport photo.
  • You trust ZKPassport's app. Its code and verifier are open source, and the ledger re-checks every proof. It is still a dependency, and we name it.

Why we accept those costs

A poll that weighs a voice by a balance lets the coins speak. This one lets the person speak, whatever they hold, even if they hold nothing. A count of passport holders, with its gaps stated, is a count of people. Passports are the first way we found to get one.

Passports are one proof of personhood, not the only one. If a proof exists that is more inclusive and as hard to forge, we want to hear about it. The ledger can host a second registry next to this one.

What you are not asked

  • Whether you hold ZEC, or how much.
  • Your name, nationality, age or address.
  • An account anywhere.

One registration, every poll

You register once. Every poll on this ledger uses the same registration, with a different pseudonym each time, so nothing links your ballots across polls. Keep the key your browser made, as a passkey or 12 words. It lets you vote in the next poll from another device.

One person, one vote

What do the people of Zcash think?

Zcash polls count coins: a ballot weighs the ZEC behind it. Zcash Agora counts people: one person, one vote, cast in private. Your ballot is sealed until the close by a clock nobody here controls, opened under a pseudonym that links to no one, and recounted by anyone. The people's count is shown next to the coins' count. Neither decides. Both inform.

Vote in the current poll   How it works

Proof of personhood is, for now, a passport verified by ZKPassport on your phone. It is one way to prove you are a person; the registry takes others as they come.

Why a second count

Take Zcash Shielded Assets. The coinholder poll of February 2026 is quoted as "98.6% against", a true sentence about the ZEC that voted. The forum's running tally counted 197 ballots on that question two days before the close, each one a voting key, not a person. The same forum has 14,742 registered members, 842 of them active in the past month. Whatever the people think about ZSAs, that poll did not ask them.

Both counts are legitimate. Coinholders carry the risk of the currency, and their view is worth measuring well. Zcash's roots are also cypherpunk, where the protections are for persons, equally, whatever they hold. A decision that looks at one count only is blind in one eye. The full argument.

This is the other half of the picture, measured with at least the same rigour, and in private: every registration, every ballot, every refusal and the result are in a public log that anyone can recount, and no ballot can be read before the close by anyone.

How a vote goes

1 · Prove you are one person, once.Scan your passport with the ZKPassport app. Only "valid passport" and a salted unique number leave your phone.
2 · Vote behind the curtain.Your browser proves you are on the list without saying who, and seals your choice to the closing time.
3 · The clock opens the ballots.At the close the drand network publishes its round; every ballot opens under its pseudonym.
4 · Anyone recounts.The log is public and timestamped in Bitcoin. Your browser can redo every check.

One person, one vote

What do the people of Zcash think?

Zcash polls count coins: a ballot weighs the ZEC behind it. Zcash Agora counts people: one person, one vote, cast in private. Your ballot is sealed until the close by a clock nobody here controls, opened under a pseudonym that links to no one, and recounted by anyone. The people's count is shown next to the coins' count. Neither decides. Both inform.

This is a test. The first poll is a trial of the technology, not a decision on anything. Its real question is: would you use a tool like this to be counted as a person in Zcash? Register, vote, recount, and tell us what you think. Nothing here binds anyone.

Proof of personhood is, for now, a passport verified by ZKPassport on your phone. It is one way to prove you are a person; the registry takes others as they come.

How a vote goes

1 · Prove you are one person, once.Scan your passport with the ZKPassport app. Only "valid passport" and a salted unique number leave your phone.
2 · Vote behind the curtain.Your browser proves you are on the list without saying who, and seals your choice to the closing time.
3 · The clock opens the ballots.At the close the drand network publishes its round; every ballot opens under its pseudonym.
4 · Anyone recounts.The log is public and timestamped in Bitcoin. Your browser can redo every check.

The first poll is a test. It asks a sample question, chosen because it has the clearest coinholder answer and the least known human one: do you want Zcash Shielded Assets in the protocol? Open from 16 November to 14 December 2026, 12:00 UTC. Its result decides nothing: the point is to see whether people in the Zcash ecosystem want to be counted this way, and whether the mechanism holds up with real passports, real phones and a real recount. If it does, later polls can take up the questions of the moment, such as the future of Zcash Community Grants or the dev fund.

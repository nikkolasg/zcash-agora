Your vote stays secret.
Even from us.

Four simple ideas, and anyone can check they hold.

Your passport stays on your phone.

The ledger receives only valid passport and a unique number

{{anim:passport}}

The ZKPassport app reads the chip and proves, on your phone, that the passport is real. Your name, photo and nationality never leave it. The number only stops the same passport from registering twice.

Your ballot travels incognito.

{{anim:ballot-path}}

Your browser proves you are on the list without saying who you are. It signs the ballot with a pseudonym and posts it to public relays run by others. Our server reads the relays and never sees your address.

Sealed until the close, by an independent clock.

{{anim:count}}

Each ballot is locked to a future round of the drand clock, run by Cloudflare, EPFL, Kudelski and others who have nothing to do with Zcash. The key does not exist before the close, so nobody can read a ballot early, us included.

Anyone can recount everything.

{{anim:pipe}}

Every registration, ballot and result goes into a public log, signed and dated in Bitcoin. Your browser can redo every check.

Audit the vote

What this means for you

Nothing about your passport is learned.Only that it is valid, and a number that even the state that issued it cannot recognize.
Nobody in Zcash can open your vote.The ballots open at the closing time, when drand publishes its round, and not before.
Your vote carries a pseudonym, not you.Nothing links it to your passport, or to your pseudonym in another poll.
Our server never sees your address.Ballots go through public relays. We read them there.

What you should know

Ballots are readable at the close.Each one opens under its pseudonym. That is what lets anyone recount. It shows a choice, never a person.
Your 12 words can prove your vote.Whoever holds them can show how you voted. Keep them to yourself.
It takes a chip passport and an NFC phone.Passports are the first proof of personhood we accept. Others can follow.
Registering takes a face match.The app checks a selfie against the passport photo, on your phone.

And it binds no one. A poll here is a measurement, offered to the people who decide.

Your vote stays secret.
Even from us.

Four simple ideas, and anyone can check they hold.

Your passport stays on your phone.

The ledger receives only valid passport and a unique number

The ZKPassport app reads the chip and proves, on the phone, that the passport is real and unexpired. Your name, photo, birth date and nationality never leave it. The number lets the same passport register once, and says nothing else. One person, one vote.

Your ballot travels incognito.

Your browser proves you are on the list without saying who, signs the ballot with a pseudonym derived from a secret only your device holds, and posts it to public Nostr relays run by others. Your address stays there: the ledger reads the sealed ballot among the rest.

Sealed until the close. By a clock, not a person.

Each ballot is locked to a future round of the drand randomness beacon, run by Cloudflare, EPFL, Kudelski, Protocol Labs and others. The key does not exist before the closing time, so nobody can peek, including us. At the close anyone opens the ballots, each under its pseudonym, and counts.

Anyone can recount everything.

✓Voters ✓Relays ✓Ledger ✓Log ✓Results

Every registration, every ballot, every refusal and the result go into a public log, signed and dated in Bitcoin. Your browser can redo every check, from the first registration to the result.

What this means for you

Nothing about your passport is learned.A proof that it is valid, and a number unique to it. Not your name, not your nationality, not your face.
Nobody can open your vote alone.Not us, not a committee: the ballots open at the closing time, when the drand network publishes its round, and not before.
Your vote carries a pseudonym, not you.The pseudonym comes from a secret on your device. Nothing links it to your passport, or to your pseudonym in another poll.
The server does not even see your address.Ballots go through public relays. The ledger reads them there, without knowing where they came from.
The limits, frankly
  • After the close, every ballot is readable under its pseudonym. That is what lets anyone recount with no key holder; it reveals a choice without a person.
  • Your 12 words let you prove your vote to someone after the close. A vote buyer could demand them. Keep them to yourself.
  • You need a passport with a chip and a phone with NFC. Passports are our first proof of personhood; the registry format takes others.
  • The issuing state can recompute your passport's number and learn that it registered, never how it voted. ZKPassport also offers a salted number that even the state cannot recompute; we are evaluating it.
  • Polls here bind no one: it is a measurement offered to the people who decide.

The NU7.1 poll, one person, one vote

Zcash coinholders and community panels are being asked five questions about Zcash Community Grants, the Dev Fund and the block time. The same five are asked here, word for word, with the same answers: Support, Oppose, Abstain.

  1. Do you support the Wind Down Zcash Community Grants proposal?
  2. Do you support the Future Dev and Legal Defense Fund proposal?
  3. Do you support the Zcash Adoption Pool (ZAP) proposal?
  4. Do you support maintaining ZCG's existing funding and operations?
  5. Assuming it can be done safely in a network upgrade, do you support reducing Zcash's target block time from 25 seconds to 20 seconds?

Register once with your passport, answer the five on one ballot, and cast. The poll closes on 30 November at 23:59 UTC. The results appear here at the close, and the number of voters stays hidden until then.

Register and vote How it works

This count is unofficial and decides nothing. It shows how many people stand behind each answer, beside the count of coins.

Why a second count

Ten ballots carried 46% of the weight.The 98.6% against ZSAs is a share of the ZEC that voted: 7% of the supply, in 204 ballots.
The people have not been counted.A Foundation panel of 99 voted 70% the other way. The forum has 842 active members. Nobody counts people.
Both counts are fair.Coinholders carry the risk of the currency. Zcash's roots protect people equally, whatever they hold.
Neither decides. Both inform.The people's count goes next to the coins' count, in a public log anyone can recount.

Why Zcash needs this

How a vote goes

1 · Prove you are one person, once.Scan your passport with the ZKPassport app. Only "valid passport" and a unique number leave your phone.
2 · Vote behind the curtain.Your browser proves you are on the list without saying who, and seals your choice until the close.
3 · The clock opens the ballots.At the close the drand network publishes its round, and every ballot opens under a pseudonym.
4 · Anyone recounts.The log is public and dated in Bitcoin. Your browser can redo every check.

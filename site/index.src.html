How a vote goes

1 · Prove you are one person, once.Scan your passport with the ZKPassport app. Only "valid passport" and a unique number leave your phone.
2 · Vote behind the curtain.Your browser proves you are on the list without saying who, and seals your choice until the close.
3 · The clock opens the ballots.At the close the drand network publishes its round, and every ballot opens under a pseudonym.
4 · Anyone recounts.The log is public and dated in Bitcoin. Your browser can redo every check.
